Our Story

Built on
Trust & Precision

Clarum was built on a different model. Not a firm chasing volume, but a boutique practice that works with a deliberately small number of clients, each selected for their ambition, complexity, and fit.

It began at Sundaram & Srinivasan, a firm built on ethics, standards, and the kind of rigorous training that shapes professionals for life. A decade of audit, taxation, and corporate compliance across banking and financial institutions, manufacturing enterprises, and closely-held companies. That breadth of sector exposure, understanding how fundamentally different industries account, report, and operate — built the foundation for everything that followed.

Then came two decades of executive experience across multiple Walt Disney companies, navigating the financial and business complexity of one of the world's most admired organizations, known for its ethics, values, and an unwavering commitment to outcomes that work for all stakeholders.

We go beyond number crunching. We embed ourselves in our clients' businesses, attending the meetings that matter, asking the questions others don't, and making decisions with the same care as an owner would.
